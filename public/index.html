Product & platform

The workshop and the machinery.

One section, two halves. The product is where your team works. The platform is what makes the output trustworthy, reproducible and safe to ship.

01 · The product
  • Live consoleChat your way from brief to twelve workstreams, switch open-source models, regenerate at a new revision.
  • Kiln mobileInstallable app for iOS, Android and desktop, with the console, chat, workstreams, sandbox and approvals.
  • Artifact studioTyped, readable deliverables — not walls of text. Approve, revise, export.
  • Isolated sandboxA working prototype and repository scaffold per user, with dev, test and production gates.
  • Proposal studioCommercial proposals, one-pagers, battlecards and executive summaries from the same evidence.
  • FDE benchBook a consultation, see the pod, and receive a structured brief before the first call.
02 · The platform
  • Seeded generation engineBrief-aware domain inference, tier profiles and reproducible artifacts at every revision.
  • Private model gatewayOpen-source models with routing, metering, prompt registries and cost controls.
  • Connector marketplacePick evidence and system connectors; approval and provenance on every source.
  • Evaluation & guardrailsSchema checks, evidence requirements, confidence and escalation to a human FDE.
  • Evidence log & policy gatesAppend-only decisions, SBOMs, signed artifacts and two-person rules for infrastructure.
