2. Cookies and local storage

Kiln uses one essential, HttpOnly session cookie to keep you signed in. The marketing site stores your readiness answers, drafting idea, attribution and a random analytics session id in browser storage. We do not use advertising cookies or sell personal data.

3. How we use data

  • Operate the workspace, generate artifacts and maintain approval and deployment evidence.
  • Authenticate you, verify email addresses, reset passwords and prevent brute-force or abusive traffic.
  • Meter token usage, enforce plan limits, process subscriptions and produce invoices.
  • Respond to enquiries, route FDE consultations, send transactional messages and, if you opt in, lifecycle product email.
  • Measure aggregate funnel performance, debug failures and improve the product.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms.

4. Legal bases

Where GDPR or similar law applies, we rely on contract performance for the workspace, legitimate interests for security, analytics and product improvement, consent for optional lifecycle email, and legal obligation for tax and accounting records. You may withdraw consent at any time.

5. Sharing and subprocessors

We share only what is necessary with infrastructure and service providers. Depending on configuration, these may include Vercel (hosting), Stripe (payments and billing portal), Resend or a configured email webhook (transactional email), and a configured CRM webhook. Current providers and regions are listed in the Data Processing Addendum. We do not sell personal data.

6. Retention

Account and workspace data is retained while your account is active, then for up to 30 days in backups unless a longer period is required by law. Lead records are retained for up to 24 months. Analytics events are capped and rotated. Invoices and tax records are retained as required by applicable law. Because the deployed demo uses ephemeral serverless storage, demo data may be reset when an instance is replaced.

7. Security

Passwords are hashed with scrypt. Sessions are HttpOnly and SameSite. We apply CSP, HSTS and related headers, rate limits and account lockout, sanitise stored labels, and gate the build blueprint behind authentication. No system is perfectly secure; report suspected issues to bidyut.banerjee@gmail.com.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, export, restriction or objection, and you may withdraw consent. Email bidyut.banerjee@gmail.com. We will verify your identity and respond within the period required by law. You may also complain to your local supervisory authority.

9. International transfers

Kiln is operated from India and uses providers that may process data in the United States or the European Economic Area. Where required, transfers rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or an equivalent mechanism described in the DPA.

10. Children

Kiln is a business product and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect their personal data.

11. Changes

We may update this policy as the product or law changes. Material changes will be announced in the console or by email. The effective date above always reflects the current version.
