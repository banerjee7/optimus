Trust · security

Security you can inspect.

Kiln is a human-gated AI delivery workspace. This page states what is implemented, what is independently assessed, and what is not. It intentionally avoids certification claims we cannot support.

Last reviewed 24 October 2026 · Security contact security@kiln.studio · Machine-readable posture: /api/trust

Controls in the product

ImplementedLeast-privilege accessServer-owned sessions, workspace RBAC (owner/admin/member/viewer), scoped API keys and SCIM provisioning.
ImplementedMFA and enterprise SSOTOTP MFA with recovery codes and OIDC single sign-on with PKCE, nonce and signed-token validation.
ImplementedTenant isolationProjects, artifacts, comments, exports and sandbox previews are authorized against workspace membership.
ImplementedAudit evidenceWorkspace, billing and Revenue Swarm evidence records include named human approvals.
ImplementedBackups and recoveryPrivate Blob snapshots with SHA-256 checksums, 20-snapshot retention and a tested restore runbook.
ImplementedSigned webhooksOutbound events use HMAC-SHA256 signatures with retries and delivery records.
In progressSOC 2 Type IIReadiness controls are documented. An independent SOC 2 report is not yet available.
Not certifiedISO 27001Kiln does not hold an ISO 27001 certificate. No certificate is claimed.

Status and resilience

Live component status is available at /api/status. The monthly uptime target for paid workspaces is 99.9%. Backups are created by an administrator or scheduled job and can be restored from the Operations view. Restore validates the snapshot checksum and takes a pre-restore safety snapshot first.

Vulnerability disclosure

Email security@kiln.studio with reproduction steps and impact. We acknowledge reports within two business days. Please do not access other customers’ data, run destructive tests, or disclose an unresolved issue publicly before we have had a reasonable opportunity to fix it. A machine-readable policy is at /.well-known/security.txt.
